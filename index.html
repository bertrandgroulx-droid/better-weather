    Why Better Weather?

    A phone-first weather app built to answer “what do I actually need to know?” at a glance — one screen, no clutter, no account, no ads.

    • 📱One screen, portrait-first. The whole picture fits without scrolling.
    • 🗣️Plain-language outlook. “Rain likely around 3 PM,” from the hourly data. When it’s dry, it counts days until rain returns — the next day with a ≥50% chance or ≥1 mm total. A “+” (e.g. “6+ days”) means that day has ≥1 mm but under 50% chance, so it likely stays dry longer.
    • ↔️Swipe through time. Hourly (48 h back, 72 h ahead) and daily (7 days back, 16 ahead) strips — swipe on a phone, or click-and-drag on a desktop.
    • 📈Tap an hour or a day. Opens a Conditions chart — that day’s hourly temperature and feels-like as two lines, with a day picker to swipe across. A fixed centre cursor reads the exact temperature, feels-like, and rain (amount & chance) for the hour under it; a precip band sits below.
    • 📍Find any place. Search a city or street address, or open the map and tap anywhere to forecast that exact spot. It reopens the last place you viewed.
    • 👆Tap for more. A small + in the margin marks the summary lines you can open — Wind, Air Quality, Moon, and Daylight.
    • 💧Precipitation you can see. Water-fill bars scaled to amount, tinted by probability.
    • 🌬️Wind & gusts. The summary shows the current wind and gust with an arrow pointing the way it blows. Tap the “Wind” line for an hourly graph — Light→Severe bands, a “now” marker, and a draggable readout for any hour.
    • 🫁Air quality. A colour-coded air-quality index — Canada’s AQHI (1–10) for Canadian locations, the US AQI everywhere else. Tap the “Air Quality” line on the summary for the details — a scale showing where your reading sits among all the categories, what the category means, and a breakdown of pollutants like PM2.5 and ozone.
    • 🌙Handcrafted details. Live moon phase (tap it for the next six months of new & full moons), a custom fog icon, aligned numbers, day markers.
    • ☀️Daylight through the year. Tap the “Daylight” line for a whole-year chart of the daylight band widening toward summer and pinching toward winter. Drag across it to read any day’s sunrise, sunset, and length. Sun times are computed on-device, so it works offline for any place.
    • 🗺️Live radar map. Animated precipitation you can scrub back through time.
    • 🔓Free & private. Powered by Open-Meteo — no keys, no tracking, no sign-in.
    • 📲Add to your home screen. Runs full-screen like an app, works offline from the last forecast, and quietly updates itself to the latest version.
    • How the forecast works
    • 🕰️History. Recent past days are the model’s best estimate; days older than ~5 days switch to observation-based reanalysis — what actually happened. (Chance-of-rain stays model-based.)
    • 📐An area, not a pinpoint. Each forecast covers a small area — typically 1–3 km across near towns, larger in remote spots — not one exact address. The yellow circle on the Map marks it.
    Data sources
    Weather & air quality from Open-Meteo, with ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis for older history.
    Radar imagery from RainViewer · Map & address search from Mapbox.
